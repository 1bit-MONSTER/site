1bit engine: local LLMs on Strix Halo

One binary to rule them all: model-agnostic local LLM inference on the AMD Ryzen AI MAX+ 395 (Strix Halo), any model on the Radeon 8060S iGPU and the XDNA 2 NPU, inside AMD's Lemonade Server. Boot a stick and it serves.
